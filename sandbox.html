HOW TO READ THE MODEL

Carbon balance is more than a single number.

Carbon enters the atmosphere from human and natural sources. Forests, oceans and soils remove part of that carbon. The sandbox compares these two sides and visualises the resulting environmental pressure.

MODEL RELATIONSHIP Net Balance = Emissions − Absorption This is an educational normalised model, not a scientific forecasting system.
