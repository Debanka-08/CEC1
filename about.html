About Carbon Footprint Calculator

🌍 What is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases (including carbon dioxide and methane) that are generated by our actions. It's measured in tonnes of CO2 equivalent per year. Every activity we do—from driving a car to using electricity—contributes to our carbon footprint.

Understanding your carbon footprint is the first step toward reducing it and helping combat climate change. The average global carbon footprint is about 4 tonnes per person per year, but in India, it's approximately 1.9 tonnes per person per year.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to empower individuals and households to understand their environmental impact and take meaningful action toward sustainability. By providing an easy-to-use calculator, we help you:

  • Measure your annual carbon emissions accurately
  • Identify the biggest contributors to your footprint
  • Track your progress over time
  • Learn practical ways to reduce emissions
  • Contribute to a healthier planet for future generations

📊 Why It Matters

Climate change is one of the most pressing challenges of our time. By calculating and reducing our carbon footprint, we can:

  • Slow global warming: Reduce greenhouse gas concentrations in the atmosphere
  • Protect ecosystems: Preserve biodiversity and natural habitats
  • Improve air quality: Create healthier living environments
  • Save money: Lower energy costs through efficiency
  • Set an example: Inspire others to take action
