🌍 Key Climate Facts

Global Emissions

Global CO2 emissions reached approximately 37 billion tonnes in recent years. The average carbon footprint per person globally is about 4 tonnes per year, but this varies significantly by country.

India's Carbon Footprint

The average Indian carbon footprint is approximately 1.9 tonnes CO2 per person per year, which is below the global average. However, rapid urbanization and industrialization are increasing emissions.

Impact of Trees

A mature tree can absorb approximately 21.77 kg of CO2 per year. Forests act as carbon sinks, storing about 296 gigatonnes of carbon globally.

Paris Agreement Target

To limit global warming to 1.5°C, we need to reduce global emissions by about 45% by 2030 and reach net-zero by 2050. Every action counts!
